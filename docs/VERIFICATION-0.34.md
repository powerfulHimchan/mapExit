# MapExit 0.34.0 — luck, consecutive attacks, upgrades and physical danger cues

Normal attacks can trigger one additional strike when agility is at least 40 and luck is positive. The bounded chance increases with both stats. Both hits roll hit/critical/proc outcomes separately, stop immediately after a kill, consume one player turn and grant one victory reward. Strong attacks never chain. Both strike animations, enemy HP changes and damage popups play in order; input and victory results wait until the whole sequence ends.

Real dungeon encounters can rarely begin with the monster's turn (about 2% at starting stats, minimum 0.1%). Agility and luck reduce the risk. Players get the ordinary guard/dodge choice before that attack; practice/direct test battles retain their existing starting turn. Luck also helps hit, dodge, flee, favorable item tiers, greater potions, chest bonuses, rare shop books and NPC gifts, and reduces monster criticals, mimics and forge failures. Existing critical/attack procs, full guards and loot bonuses remain. Map geometry, NPC identities and skill-based obstacle answers remain deterministic or player-controlled. The beggar no longer advertises a potion probability; a separate stable bonus stream makes current luck improve even a previously cached non-gift, with no repeat reward.

New consumable: 강화서 (upgrade book), with distinct parchment art. Choose an equippable inventory target and preview +1 before spending one book. Preserve the real target ID, equipped slots, inheritance, boss affixes and negative tradeoffs; improve positive stats. Necklace refinements improve their actual special effect. Books cannot be equipped, inherited or quick-equipped. Completed quests guarantee one book; 익숙한 사냥꾼 and 믿음직한 해결사 achievements add one. Merchants occasionally stock one, at 2.5–6% depending on luck; stock is saved and not rerolled on loading.

Forges appear on floors 20 and 40, including repair of older saved maps. One forge per floor, repeatable access. Two matching items of the same +grade yield one of the next grade; boss options must also match. Confirmation shows both actual items, equipped state, resulting stats and odds. Success keeps the base ID and equipment slots; failure consumes only the material and preserves the base. Inherited equipment is never consumed as material. Failure is 10% at zero luck and falls to a 2% minimum. Invalid/stale actions cannot consume anything. Item IDs and save version remain compatible.

Cauldron art uses a deep bowl and rotated rim, a continuous stream from the lowered lip to the actual opposite danger lane, timed falling droplets and a floor splash. Success/failure keep the final correct danger lane, and the existing final step/center-return motion remains. At HP >0 and <=10%, a red aura/exclamation surrounds the character, a slow scene-edge pulse appears and a readable warning is shown; recovery clears it.

Validation covers consecutive hits and one-turn/one-reward limits, strong/low-stat exclusions, rare first turns and saves, gift distributions/one-use behavior, forge placement/repair, +2→+3→+4 recipes, failure and inherited/equipped IDs, upgrade books and actual necklace effects, and opposite cauldron lanes. Android scenarios cover book use/reload, forge confirmation/success/failure on normal/compact screens, both attack animation frames/input lock and low-HP/cauldron graphics. Full core tests, atlas consistency, Android lint, debug assembly and device regressions run before merge.
